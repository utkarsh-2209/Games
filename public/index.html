How Ultimate 9×9 Works

The board consists of 9 mini-boards arranged in a 3×3 grid. Each mini-board has 9 cells.

The Core Rule:
Whichever micro cell you pick determines the macro mini-board your opponent must play in next!

🎯 Wildcard Free Move: If sent to a board that is already won or full, you can play in any open mini-board!

🏆 Winning: Win 3 mini-boards in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to win the entire game!

↩️ Undo Requests: In online multiplayer, clicking Request Undo sends an alert to your opponent. If they accept, your last move is rolled back!

Rules of Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe

Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe consists of a large 3×3 grid of 9 small Tic-Tac-Toe boards, totaling 81 individual squares.

How Moves Direct the Game:
Each move is made inside one mini-board. The exact cell you choose in that mini-board dictates which mini-board the next player must play in!
Example: If you place your mark in the top-right square of any board, your opponent's next move MUST be placed somewhere in the top-right mini-board.

🌟 Wildcard / Free Move: If you are sent to a mini-board that has already been won or is tied/full, you are granted a Free Move and can play in ANY unfinished mini-board!

🏅 Winning a Mini-Board: Getting 3-in-a-row inside a mini-board claims that board for your symbol ('X' or 'O').

🏆 Winning the Ultimate Match: Be the first player to win 3 mini-boards in a row on the main grid (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal)!

↩️ The Undo System: You can click "Request Undo" at any time. In multiplayer, your opponent is prompted to accept or reject the undo. If accepted, the board seamlessly rewinds!
